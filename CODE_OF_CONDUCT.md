# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, welcome newcomers, and keep discussion technical and
constructive. Harassment, discrimination, and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Report concerns to the maintainers via a private GitHub Security Advisory or direct message.
Maintainers will review and respond confidentially and may remove content or contributors who
violate these norms.
